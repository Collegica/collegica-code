# Datasets

* [Bank of Canada overnight rate, monthly, 2021–2026](overnight.md) - Sixty-nine observations — the first of each month from January 2021 to September 2026 — of the Bank of Canada's target for the overnight rate, from the Valet API series V39079.
* [Rate inputs for the mortgage simulation](rates.md) - Bank of Canada overnight-rate changes by effective date, the big-bank prime spread, and the best insured rates on offer at fifteen signing months from June 2021 to September 2026 — each sourced line by line in the historical-rates note.
