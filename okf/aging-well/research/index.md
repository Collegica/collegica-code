# Research Reports

* [A session you can keep — plan and research](exercise-sessions.md) - What was verified and against what, the decisions behind the routine and the app, what was judgement rather than evidence, and the traps hit generating the clips.
* [Finding the next job, with AI — research report and series plan](next-job-with-ai.md) - Four research streams synthesised: the argument in five lines, the numbers on duration and pay after 45, what the employer's AI actually does, and the plan to build on resume-typst. Canadian, Ontario-first.

# Research Notes

* [Existing coverage of AI-assisted job search for older workers](existing-coverage.md) - Around thirty-five pieces surveyed, what each owns, and the gaps that map onto habits Collegica had already published.
* [One dead bug, five video models — cost and form](exercise-clips.md) - The same prompt to five text-to-video models: credits per clip, what each produced, and the finding that price did not predict form and that the failure mode is a clean performance of a different exercise.
* [Open-source AI job-search tools on GitHub](github-open-source.md) - Around thirty repositories surveyed — auto-appliers, résumé tailoring, ATS matching — and which ones refuse to auto-submit.
* [Routines — the families, the session, the sources](routines.md) - The anti-movement, loaded and on-the-feet families with their moves; the five-block session and its 30, 45 and 60 minute arithmetic; what is in the library and what was left out.
* [The landscape: labour-market facts, what the ATS really does, and the Ontario rules](landscape.md) - Primary sources on long-term unemployment after 45, re-employment pay, ATS and knockout questions, and the Ontario AI-disclosure requirement.
* [X discourse: AI in the job hunt, mid-career and older workers](x-discourse.md) - Around twenty fetched posts with engagement figures, the Workday age-discrimination case, and a viral post identified as satire.
