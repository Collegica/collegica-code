Small, local-first tools that ship as pages on the site: two built (the OWL Planner, in its own repository, and OWL Sessions, in this one) and three specced but not built, each because an article commits to it.

# Applications

* [OWL Planner](owl-planner.md) - A Pyodide budget tool that reads bank exports and PDF statements entirely in the browser, reconciled against the statements' own totals. Collegica's first shipped app, in its own repository.
* [OWL Sessions](owl-sessions.md) - Thirty, forty-five or sixty minute exercise sessions from a library of seventeen generated clips, each move shown under a timer and a rep count, a minute of rest between blocks, everything in the page. The muscle tool beside OWL Planner.

# Specifications

* [Funnel](funnel.md) - The weekly review of an applications folder as a funnel, from the article's one-subfolder-per-job convention — specced, not built.
* [Morning digest](morning-digest.md) - A daily digest of postings worth a targeted application, filtered by your own criteria — specced, not built.
* [Parse-and-gap](parse-and-gap.md) - A local tool that parses a résumé the way an ATS does and shows the gap against a posting — specced, not built.
