# OWL Sessions

Moved on 2026-09-29 to its own public repository,
**[Collegica/owl-sessions](https://github.com/Collegica/owl-sessions)**: the
app, its clips, the prompts they were made from, `fetch.sh` and the contact
sheets (in its `library/`). It is served at
[collegica.org/apps/owl-sessions/](https://www.collegica.org/apps/owl-sessions/),
at the release pinned in [`website/apps.yml`](../../website/apps.yml).

What stays here is private: `jobs.json`, the HiggsField job ids that map each
clip to the generation that made it, and the full-size clips in `videos/`
(gitignored).
