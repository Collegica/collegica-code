An AI article for small businesses built on Lauren Lucas's interview on
RealDealCrew (27 Oct 2026), generalised from real estate to any small
business, with every claim checked and the Canadian rules added. Started
2026-10-28. Draft: `website/ai/small-business-ai/index.md` (`draft: true`).

## Decisions

1. **Generalise, keep her examples.** The lessons are stated for any small
   business; her real estate examples stay as examples.
2. **Her numbers are hers.** 200+ consultations / 187 shows are attributed,
   not verified. The video promotes her paid community and the host's firm;
   the article says so.
3. **Add what the video omits:** CASL (DMs are covered; 6-month and 2-year
   implied consent; consent requests are themselves CEMs), PIPEDA
   accountability and cross-border notice, Quebec Law 25, Meta's DM rules.
4. **ChatGPT plan details are left general.** Every OpenAI page refused
   automated access; only the API code-interpreter doc was read.
5. **"End of 2027" is presented as opinion.**

## Prices in CAD (added 2026-10-28, on review)

Costs are shown in Canadian dollars, converted at the Bank of Canada rate of
28 October 2026 (FXUSDCAD 1.4036, Valet API). Claude's price was read from
claude.com/pricing in a browser in Canada ($20 monthly, $17 annual; the page
names no currency, treated as USD). HubSpot, Cal.com and Calendly free tiers
were read on their pricing pages. ChatGPT and ManyChat prices are from search
snippets only: openai/chatgpt.com and manychat.com refused automated access,
and the browser's host policy blocked reading them; the article says so.
Paid CRM and scheduler tiers were dropped from the cost table, since the
free tiers cover a one-person business.
