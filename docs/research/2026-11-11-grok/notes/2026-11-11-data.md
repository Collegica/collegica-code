## grok.com and the phone apps (xAI's own policy)

- **Training.** "We may use your content and interactions with Grok (e.g.,
  prompts, searches, and other materials you submit) along with Grok's
  responses to train our models … You control whether your data is used for
  training Grok." (Consumer FAQs)
- **The switch.** Phone app: Settings > Data Controls > "Improve the model".
  grok.com: Settings > Data > "Improve the Model". "Once you select that your
  data should not be used for training, your new conversations will not be
  used to train our models." The FAQ does not say whether it is on by
  default.
- **Feedback.** "even if you opt out of model training, when you
  subsequently decide to voluntarily provide feedback, that feedback may be
  used for training purposes."
- **Not signed in.** "in some regions (excluding the EU/UK), when you use
  Grok without logging in, you won't have the option to opt out of model
  training."
- **Private Chat** (the ghost icon, top right): not kept in history, not
  used for training, "deleted from SpaceXSI systems within 30 days" (FAQ and
  policy, §5).
- **Deleting.** A chat or all chats or the account; "it will take up to 30
  days to delete from SpaceXSI systems" unless de-identified or kept "for
  safety, security, or legal reasons".
- **People reading chats.** "A limited number of our authorized personnel
  may review your conversations with Grok for specific business purposes,
  including improving model performance".
- **Personalisation from X.** Settings > Data > "Personalize Grok using X".
- **Google connectors.** "SpaceXSI shall not use any Google Apps content for
  any of its internal AI or other training purposes" (policy, §2).
- **Business plans.** "we do not use content from our business and
  enterprise customers to improve our models" (FAQ).
- **Build mode on grok.com.** "chats and build sessions are retained for
  product functionality"; Private Chat is not available for Build mode; with
  "improve the model" on, build sessions "including data from created apps,
  may be used for product and model improvements" (developer FAQ).

## Grok in X (X's policy, not xAI's)

- "X may share with xAI your public X data as well as your user
  interactions, inputs and results with Grok on X to train and fine-tune
  Grok and other generative AI models." Public data means public posts,
  their metadata, public Spaces and public profiles.
- **The switch.** X settings > Privacy & Safety > Data sharing and
  personalization > Grok & Third-party Collaborators > "Data Sharing":
  "Allow your public data as well as your interactions, inputs, and results
  with Grok and xAI to be used for training and fine-tuning."
- The opt-out "does not prevent a deployed model from learning as a result
  of its normal use" in X features powered by Grok.
- A separate switch, "Grok Personalization", in the same place.
- Making your account private ("Protect your posts") keeps posts out of
  training.
- **Deleting.** Privacy & Safety > Data sharing and personalization > Grok >
  "Delete Conversation History"; removed "within 30 days".
- **Group chats.** Adding @Grok to an encrypted X Chat gives X and xAI "access
  to all the messages sent in that conversation after Grok is added".

## Grok Build (the command-line app)

- `/privacy` opens "Coding data, retention, and training", **Opt in** or
  **Opt out** (local user guide 04). docs.x.ai: "You can disable code data
  retention with these settings, even if ZDR is not enabled", and the app
  shows "a screen to allow us to retain data to improve the product and
  model" on first use.
- Telemetry is a separate switch (`DISABLE_TELEMETRY=1`).
- Session transcripts are kept on the computer under `~/.grok/sessions/`.
- No page read today says in so many words what Grok Build sends. The model
  runs on xAI's servers (sign-in is to grok.com, or an API key for
  api.x.ai), so what the agent reads goes there to be processed, unless you
  configure a model of your own (`[model.<name>]` with a `base_url`,
  docs.x.ai overview, "Custom models"). The article says this as an
  inference, not a quotation.
