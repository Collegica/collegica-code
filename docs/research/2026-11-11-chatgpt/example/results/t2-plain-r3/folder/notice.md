# Alder Lane Community Garden: autumn work day

Dear members,

Please join us at the garden on **Saturday 14 November 2026, from 9:30 am to 12:30 pm**, for our autumn work day as we prepare for the winter closure at the end of November.

Please bring gloves. The club will provide soup, and a tool sharpener is booked to arrive at **10:00 am**.

**The water will be shut off for the winter on the work day.** Please plan any watering needs accordingly.

Thank you for helping look after our garden. We look forward to seeing you there!

The Alder Lane Community Garden Committee
