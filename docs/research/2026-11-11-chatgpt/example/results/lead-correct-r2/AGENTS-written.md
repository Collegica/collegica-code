# House rules for member communications

Apply these rules to anything written for members:

- Write dates in full, including the weekday and year, for example Saturday 14 November 2026.
- Use the 24-hour clock with leading zeroes, for example 09:30.
- Use British spelling.
- Do not use exclamation marks.
- Keep the entire communication under 120 words, including the heading and signature.
- Sign exactly "The committee", with no personal names.

Check every rule before saving a member communication. Use the committee's notes as the source of factual details.
