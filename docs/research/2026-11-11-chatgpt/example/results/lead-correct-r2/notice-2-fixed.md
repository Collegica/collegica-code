# Alder Lane Community Garden: autumn work day

Dear members,

Please join us at the garden on **Saturday 14 November 2026, from 09:30 to 12:30**, to help prepare for winter.

Bring your gloves. The club will provide soup, and a tool sharpener is booked to arrive at **10:00**.

Please be aware that the water will be shut off for the winter during the work day. The garden closes at the end of November.

Thank you for helping care for our shared garden. We look forward to seeing you there.
