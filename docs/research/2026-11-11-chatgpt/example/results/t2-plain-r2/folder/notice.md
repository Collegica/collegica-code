# Alder Lane Community Garden: Members’ Work Day

Dear members,

Please join us at the garden on **Saturday 14 November 2026, from 09:30 to 12:30**, for our work day as we prepare for winter.

**Bring your gloves.** The club will provide soup, and a tool sharpener will be there at **10:00**.

Please remember that **the water will be shut off for the winter during the work day**. The garden closes at the end of November.

We look forward to seeing you there and thank you for helping care for our garden.

The Alder Lane Community Garden Committee
