# Autumn work day

Please join us at Alder Lane Community Garden on Saturday 14 November 2026, from 09:30 to 12:30, for our autumn work day.

Bring gloves. Soup will be provided, and a tool sharpener will be there at 10:00.

The water will be shut off for the winter during the work day. The garden closes at the end of November.
