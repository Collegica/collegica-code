# Alder Lane Community Garden: Work Day

Dear members,

Please join us at the garden for our autumn work day on **Saturday 14 November 2026, from 09:30 to 12:30**, as we prepare for winter.

Please bring gloves. The club will provide soup, and a tool sharpener will be there at **10:00**.

The water will be shut off for the winter during the work day. The garden closes at the end of November.

Thank you for helping care for our garden. We look forward to seeing you there.

The committee
