# Alder Lane Community Garden: autumn work day

Dear members,

Please join us at the garden on **Saturday 14 November 2026, from 09:30 to 12:30**, for our autumn work day as we prepare for the garden to close at the end of November.

Please bring gloves. The club will provide soup, and a tool sharpener is booked to arrive at **10:00**.

The garden's water will be shut off for the winter during the work day, so please plan any watering needs accordingly.

Thank you for helping get the garden ready for winter.

The committee
