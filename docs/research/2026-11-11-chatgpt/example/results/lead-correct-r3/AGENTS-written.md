# House rules for member communications

Apply these rules to anything sent to members:

- Write dates in full, including the weekday and year: Saturday 14 November 2026.
- Use the 24-hour clock with two-digit hours and minutes: 09:30.
- Use British spelling.
- Do not use exclamation marks.
- Keep the entire communication under 120 words, including the heading and signature.
- Sign exactly "The committee" and include no personal names.

Before saving a member communication, check every rule above and count all words, including the heading and signature.
