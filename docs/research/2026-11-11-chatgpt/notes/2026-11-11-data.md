# Where your data goes

Read on 11 November 2026. **The weakest note in this folder.** OpenAI's
privacy and Help Centre pages (openai.com/policies, help.openai.com) returned
HTTP 403 to every tool available here, `curl` and a summarising fetch alike.
What follows is what could be read on vendor pages, plus, marked, the
excerpts a web search returned from Help Centre pages that could not be
opened. The owner should read the pages themselves before this section of the
guide is published (README, *For the owner*).

## Read directly

- **chatgpt.com/pricing**, Privacy row: "Content is used to train our models
  — Opt-out available" for Free, Go, Plus and Pro. The Business card on the
  Codex pricing page: "No training on your business data by default."
- **Authentication** (learn.chatgpt.com/docs/auth.md): "When you sign in
  with ChatGPT, Codex usage follows your ChatGPT workspace permissions,
  role-based access control (RBAC), and ChatGPT Enterprise retention and
  residency settings." "With an API key, usage follows your API
  organization's retention and data-sharing settings instead."
- **Advanced configuration**, "History persistence": "By default, Codex saves
  local session transcripts under `CODEX_HOME` (for example,
  `~/.codex/history.jsonl`)." To stop it: `[history] persistence = "none"`.
  (Session logs were also found under `~/.codex/sessions/` during the tests.)
- **Memories**: local Codex memories are stored under `~/.codex`; "Don't store
  secrets in memories."
- **Projects**: in the desktop app, "Syncing a conversation doesn't upload
  the entire local folder or make it available to another computer."
- **Config reference**, `features.apps`: "App and connector traffic is not
  controlled by the sandboxed-command network proxy or its domain allowlist."

## Seen only as search-result excerpts (not read on the page)

A web search limited to openai.com and help.openai.com, for the training
switch, returned these Help Centre articles: "How your data is used to
improve model performance" (articles/5722486), "Data Controls FAQ"
(articles/7730893), "What if I want to keep my history on but disable model
training?" (articles/8983130). The search tool's summary of them said:

- the switch is **Improve the model for everyone**, under Settings > Data
  Controls on the web;
- on personal plans the same switch "also governs Codex tasks";
- feedback (thumbs up or down) may lead to the whole conversation being used
  for training even after opting out;
- Temporary Chat: chats "won't appear in history, use or create memories, or
  be used to train our models";
- one article says the default "depends on your location and settings".

None of this goes into the guide as fact. The guide says only what the
pricing page says (content is used for training on personal plans, with an
opt-out) and tells the reader to look in the app's settings, without naming
the switch or its place; the owner is to confirm both (README, *For the
owner*). A "Data Controls" section does exist in the web app's settings: the
Projects page (read directly) says "Restore archived chats from **Settings >
Data Controls > Archived chats**". That does not show the training switch is
there.
