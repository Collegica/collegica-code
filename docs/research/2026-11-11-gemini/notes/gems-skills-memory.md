# Gems, skills, memory and instructions: notes

Gemini Apps Help pages, read 2026-11-11 as their own text.

## Gems, and their end

<https://support.google.com/gemini/answer/15146780> ("Use Gems in Gemini
Apps"), read 2026-11-11:

- Banner: "Starting in November 2026, Gems will go away on personal Google
  accounts and automatically transition to skills."
- A Gem is a name, instructions and optional "Knowledge" files (upload, or
  "Add from Drive", which needs Keep Activity on). "If you add a file from
  your Drive, Gemini will use the most recent version of the file."
- "When you create a Gem in the Gemini web app, it will appear in the Gemini
  mobile app and the Gemini side panel in Google Workspace."

<https://support.google.com/gemini/answer/18560919> ("About the transition
from Gems to skills"), read 2026-11-11:

- "Starting in November 2026, Gems will transition to skills in Gemini Apps
  for personal Google Accounts, and in 2027, for work and school accounts."
- Timetable for personal accounts 18 and over: "November 17, 2026: Gems
  transition to skills and become read only … December 17, 2026: Gems are
  removed from Gemini Apps." Workspace business accounts March 2027,
  education June 2027.
- "When we remove Gems, we'll automatically recreate your Gems as skills."
- "Some files will not carry over … you can download your files from Gems
  before December 17, 2026." Not carried: ".doc, .xls, .ppt" and archives.
  "Skills have a 100 MB total size limit for files"; if larger, "we
  automatically remove the largest files first".
- Skills: "reusable custom instructions"; used by typing "/ (soon to be @)"
  and the name; "Gemini automatically recognizes when a skill is relevant to
  your current prompt and applies it for you"; several can be stacked.
- A skill downloads as a `.zip` with a `SKILL.md`; skills made elsewhere can be
  imported "by uploading their SKILL.md files" (Mac app and web only).
- "only 100 can be active at a time".
- "they do not yet work with features like Canvas and Deep Research."
- Google's three practices for writing a skill: "Define when to use it",
  "Keep it concise", "Provide examples: Include concrete examples of the
  output you expect."
- Opal and "Gems by Google Labs" also go in November.

## Memory of past chats

<https://support.google.com/gemini/answer/16598469> ("Get personalization with
memory of your past Gemini chats"), read 2026-11-11:

- Needs a personal Google Account and Keep Activity on.
- Switched in Settings > Personal Intelligence.
- "To check if Gemini used your past chats, just ask Gemini, 'Did you use any
  info from past chats?'"
- "To delete something that Gemini remembered about you, delete all chats with
  this info from Gemini Apps Activity." For connected apps, both delete the
  chats and disconnect the app.
- Not available "in certain features, like Gems or Live chats".

## Remembered facts and "Instructions for Gemini"

<https://support.google.com/gemini/answer/16598625> ("Manage what Gemini
remembers about you and customize responses"), read 2026-11-11:

- Asking it to remember specific facts needs "a Google AI plan", a personal
  account and Memory on, and is "Only available in the US. All users can add
  instructions for Gemini Apps."
- Instructions are entered at Settings > Personal Intelligence >
  Instructions for Gemini, and can be switched off there.
- "Any items that you ask Gemini to remember, or instructions that you give
  for Gemini will last for a maximum of 18 months."
- "Instructions for Gemini to forget or avoid topics in chats doesn't always
  work perfectly. To make sure Gemini stops mentioning a topic, delete any
  chats about it from your Gemini Apps Activity."

<https://support.google.com/gemini/answer/15637730> ("Get personalization in
Gemini Apps"), read 2026-11-11: personalisation comes from memory of past
chats, connected Google apps, and "Your preferences, like instructions";
"not available while signed in to a work, school, or supervised account."
