# Where the data goes: notes

Gemini Apps Help pages, read 2026-11-11 as their own text. The Gemini Apps
Privacy Notice and Google's Privacy Policy themselves were not read; the
Generative AI in Google Workspace Privacy Hub (work and school accounts) was
not read.

## Gemini Apps Privacy Hub

<https://support.google.com/gemini/answer/13594961>, read 2026-11-11; its
sections carry "Last updated: October 26, 2026" and "October 13, 2026".

- "Google uses your activity to provide, develop, and improve its services
  (including training generative AI models), as well as to protect Google,
  its users, and the public with the help of human reviewers. Data reviewed by
  service providers is disconnected from your account and saved for 3 years."
- "Human reviewers (including trained reviewers from our service providers)
  review some of the data we collect for these purposes. Please don't enter
  confidential information that you wouldn't want a reviewer to see or Google
  to use to improve our services".
- "To stop your future chats from being reviewed to improve Google services,
  turn off your Keep Activity setting."
- "Temporary chats and chats you have when Keep Activity is off are retained
  with your account for 72 hours".
- "Temporary chats are not used to train Google's AI models. If Keep Activity
  is off and you don't submit feedback, Google also does not use your future
  chats to improve its AI models."
- "Even if your Keep Activity setting is off or you use temporary chats,
  Google still uses your chats to respond to you and help protect Google, our
  users, and the public, including with help from human reviewers."
- "Chats reviewed by human reviewers … are not deleted when you delete your
  activity. Instead, they are retained for up to three years."
- "You can change your auto-delete setting in Gemini Apps Activity from the
  default of 18 months to 3 months, 36 months, or indefinite."
- "Your audio and Gemini Live videos and screenshares aren't used to improve
  Google services by default."
- Connected apps: "other Connected Apps like Google Workspace are unavailable
  if you turn off Keep Activity".
- Work or school accounts "may be subject to different data handling terms",
  in the Workspace privacy hub.

## Gemini Apps Activity

<https://support.google.com/gemini/answer/13278892> ("Manage & delete your
activity in Gemini Apps"), read 2026-11-11:

- "If you're 18 or over, Keep Activity is on by default."
- Managed at gemini.google.com or "myactivity.google.com/product/gemini".
- "Even when Keep Activity is off, your conversations will be saved with your
  account for up to 72 hours to allow Google to provide the service and
  process any feedback."
- "By default, your Gemini Apps activity older than 18 months is
  auto-deleted. You can turn off Auto-delete or change the auto-delete period
  to 3 or 36 months."
- "If Keep Activity is off, any Gems you create will still be saved to your
  account. Any chats you create with Gems will not be saved."
- "If a shared chat is deleted, it doesn't delete the related public link."
