# Frequently asked questions (copied from our old website, March 2025)

**How many guests do you cater for?** From 15 guests up to 250.

**How far do you deliver?** Anywhere within 25 km of our kitchen at no charge.

**Do you need a deposit?** Yes, 25% when you confirm, the rest a week before the event.

**Can you do vegetarian food?** Yes, every menu has a vegetarian option.

**How do I get a quote?** Send us the date, the number of guests and the menu you like.
We reply within two business days.
