# Quotes

To get a quote, a customer sends us the date, the number of guests and the
menu they like. We reply within two business days.[^faq-2025-03]

The events coordinator sends every quote from the **shared quote template**,
not from a personal copy.[^meeting-2026-10-14]

When quoting, check the booking date against the rules that change on
1 November 2026 (minimum guests, deposit): see
[Orders and deposits](orders-and-deposits.md).
