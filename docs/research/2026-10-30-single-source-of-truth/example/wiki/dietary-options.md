# Dietary options

## Vegetarian

Every menu has a vegetarian option.[^faq-2025-03] There is also a
vegetarian working lunch on the price list, at the same price as the working
lunch (24.00 CAD per guest, minimum 15 guests until 31 October
2026).[^price-list-2026-09][^meeting-2026-10-14]

## Gluten-free

We can make the **working lunch** and the **hot buffet** gluten-free, with
**three days' notice**.[^meeting-2026-10-14]

Our kitchen handles flour, so we **cannot promise a gluten-free
environment**. Say so to anyone with coeliac disease.[^meeting-2026-10-14]

The wiki does not say whether gluten-free costs extra, or whether other menus
can be made gluten-free. (Listed in [open questions](open-questions.md).)
