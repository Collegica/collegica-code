# Orders and deposits

## Minimum and maximum guests

**Until 31 October 2026**, each menu has its own minimum number of guests,
set in the price list: 15 for the breakfast spread, the working lunch and the
vegetarian working lunch; 25 for the hot buffet; 30 for the cocktail
reception.[^price-list-2026-09] These minimums give way to the new rule
below on 1 November 2026.[^meeting-2026-10-14] See [Price list](price-list.md).

**From 1 November 2026**, "the minimum order is 20 guests for every
menu." Existing bookings are honoured.[^meeting-2026-10-14] The reason given:
small orders lose money once delivery and setup are counted.[^meeting-2026-10-14]

It is not yet confirmed whether this lowers the hot buffet (25) and cocktail
reception (30) minimums to 20, or only raises the lower ones. Until a person
confirms, don't quote a hot buffet or cocktail reception below its price-list
minimum. (Listed in [open questions](open-questions.md).)

The largest event we cater is 250 guests.[^faq-2025-03] This comes from the
old website (March 2025) and has not been restated since.

## Deposit

- **Bookings made before 1 November 2026:** 25% when you confirm.[^faq-2025-03]
- **Bookings made from 1 November 2026:** 30%.[^meeting-2026-10-14]

In both cases the balance is due one week before the event.[^faq-2025-03][^meeting-2026-10-14]

The deposit rule goes by the date the booking is made, not the date of the
event.[^meeting-2026-10-14] What counts as the booking date (enquiry,
confirmation or deposit paid) is not yet defined; see
[open questions](open-questions.md).
