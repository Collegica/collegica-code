# What are your investments costing you?

Notes behind `website/finance/investment-fees/index.md` (finance series
item 6).

- `scripts/fees.py` prints every figure in the article; `scripts/output.txt` is
  its output. All holdings, returns and one-time costs are invented
  assumptions.

## Sources read on 2026-11-25

- GetSmarterAboutMoney: mutual fund fees (MER includes trailing commissions;
  advisory fee typically 1-2% in fee-based accounts; deferred sales charges
  banned from 2022-06-01, old schedules up to seven years); understanding Fund
  Facts (MER and TER); how ETFs work; total cost reporting explainer (fund
  expense ratio = MER + TER; 2026 costs received January 2027).
- Osler (2023-04-21) and Blakes (2023-05-16): effective 2026-01-01, first
  reports for the year ending 2026-12-31; prospectus-exempt and
  labour-sponsored funds excluded; segregated funds under separate CCIR
  guidance.
