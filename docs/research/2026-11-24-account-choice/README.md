# TFSA, RRSP or FHSA, decided with arithmetic

Notes behind `website/finance/account-choice/index.md` (finance series item 2).

- `scripts/accounts.py` prints every table in the article; `scripts/output.txt`
  is its output. Pure arithmetic, round numbers, no real person's data.
- Rule: the RRSP and TFSA tie when the tax rate now equals the rate on
  withdrawal; growth cancels. The refund is not counted separately.
- Limits quoted for 2026 (checked 2026-11-24): TFSA $7,000, RRSP $33,810,
  FHSA $8,000 a year, $40,000 lifetime, carry-forward at most $8,000.
- OAS recovery tax: 15% above the threshold; $93,454 for 2025 (Canada.ca);
  $95,323 for 2026 from a third party, not confirmed on Canada.ca.

## Not verified

- The Guaranteed Income Supplement reduction rate: the article says only that
  it falls as other income rises.
- The FHSA closure time limit is described by pointer to the CRA page, not
  restated.
- Province-specific marginal rates: deliberately not used.
