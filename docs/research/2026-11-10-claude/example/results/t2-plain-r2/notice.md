# Autumn work day: Saturday 14 November

Dear members,

The autumn work day at Alder Lane Community Garden is this **Saturday, 14 November, from 09:30 to 12:30**. All members are welcome.

- **Bring gloves.**
- **Soup is provided** by the club.
- **Tool sharpening:** the tool sharpener will be at the garden from 10:00, so bring any tools you would like sharpened.
- **Compost:** the bays are full, ready for putting your beds to sleep for the winter.

**The water will be shut off for the winter on the work day.** If you need water for anything on your plot, please use it before then.

The garden closes for the winter at the end of November.

See you on Saturday,

The Alder Lane Community Garden Committee
