# Alder Lane Community Garden

## House rules for anything written to members

Apply these to every notice, email or message addressed to members:

- Write dates in full with the weekday and year, e.g. Saturday 14 November 2026.
- Write times on the 24-hour clock, e.g. 09:30, 15:00.
- Use British spelling.
- No exclamation marks.
- Keep it under 120 words.
- Sign it "The committee". Do not use personal names anywhere in the text, including when saying who is doing a task.

Check the finished text against every rule before saving.
