# Work day: Saturday 14 November 2026

Dear members,

Our autumn work day takes place on Saturday 14 November 2026, from 09:30 to 12:30.

Please bring gloves. We will supply soup for everyone.

A tool sharpener will be at the garden from 10:00, so bring any blunt tools along. The water will also be shut off for the winter during the work day.

The garden closes for the season at the end of November, and your help on the day will make that much easier.

We hope to see you there.
