# Alder Lane Community Garden: Work Day, Saturday 14 November

Dear members,

Our autumn work day is this **Saturday, 14 November, from 09:30 to 12:30**.

**What to bring:** gloves. The club will supply soup.

**On the day**
- A tool sharpener will be at the garden at 10:00. Bring along any tools that need an edge.
- Wren will shut off the water for the winter during the work day, so please don't count on the taps after that.

**Garden news**
- The shed roof has been replaced.
- The main gate latch has been fixed and the gate now closes properly.
- This autumn's compost has arrived and is in the bays.

The garden closes for the season at the end of November.

We hope to see you on Saturday.

Alder Lane Community Garden Committee
