# Alder Lane Community Garden

## House rules for anything written to members

Apply these to every notice, email or message addressed to members:

- Dates in full, with the weekday and the year: "Saturday 14 November 2026".
- Times on the 24-hour clock: "09:30", "15:00".
- British spelling (organise, colour, neighbour, programme).
- No exclamation marks.
- Under 120 words.
- Sign off as "The committee", with no personal names. Do not name individual committee members anywhere in the text, even to say who is doing a task.

Before saving a member-facing document, check it against this list, including the word count.
