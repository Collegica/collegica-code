# Alder Lane Community Garden: work day

Dear members,

Our end-of-season work day is on **Saturday 14 November 2026, from 09:30 to 12:30**.

Please bring gloves. We will provide soup.

A tool sharpener will be at the garden from 10:00, so bring any tools you would like sharpened.

The water will be shut off for the winter during the work day, so please do any watering before then. The garden closes for the season at the end of November 2026.
