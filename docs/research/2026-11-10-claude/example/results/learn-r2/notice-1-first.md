# Alder Lane Community Garden: Work Day

**Saturday 14 November, 09:30 to 12:30**

Dear members,

Our end-of-season work day is this Saturday. Please come along and help get the garden ready for winter.

**What to bring:** gloves.

**What we provide:** soup, for everyone at the end of the morning.

**Tool sharpening:** a tool sharpener will be at the garden from 10:00. Bring your own tools along if you'd like them sharpened.

**Water shut-off:** Wren will turn the water off for the winter during the work day. After Saturday the taps will be off, so please do any watering you need before then.

The garden closes for the season at the end of November.

Thank you, and we hope to see you there.

The Alder Lane Community Garden committee
