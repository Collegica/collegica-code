# Autumn work day: Saturday 14 November 2026

Dear members,

The autumn work day at Alder Lane Community Garden is on Saturday 14 November 2026, from 09:30 to 12:30. Please come and help us get the garden ready for winter.

- Bring your own gloves.
- Soup will be provided by the club.
- A tool sharpener will be at the garden from 10:00, so bring any tools that need an edge.
- The water will be shut off for the winter on the day, so there will be no water on site after that.

We look forward to seeing you there.

The committee
