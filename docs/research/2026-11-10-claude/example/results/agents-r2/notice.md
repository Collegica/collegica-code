# Autumn work day: Saturday 14 November 2026

Dear members,

The garden's autumn work day is on Saturday 14 November 2026, from 09:30 to 12:30, at Alder Lane. We will be getting the garden ready for winter, and all members are welcome.

- Please bring your own gloves.
- The club will supply soup.
- A tool sharpener will be on site from 10:00.

The water will be shut off for the winter on the day, so there will be no water at the garden after the work day.

The garden closes for the winter at the end of November.

We look forward to seeing you there.

The committee
