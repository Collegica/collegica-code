# Alder Lane Community Garden: Work Day, Saturday 14 November

Dear members,

Our autumn work day is this **Saturday, 14 November, from 09:30 to 12:30**.

**What to bring:** gloves. The club will supply soup.

**On the day:**
- A tool sharpener will be at the garden at 10:00. Bring any tools you would like sharpened.
- Wren will shut off the water for the winter during the work day.

**Recent jobs:** The shed roof has been replaced, the gate latch has been fixed, and the compost has arrived and is in the bays.

The garden closes for the season at the end of November.

We hope to see you on Saturday.

The Alder Lane Community Garden committee
