# Work day at Alder Lane Community Garden

Dear members,

Our autumn work day takes place on Saturday 14 November 2026, from 09:30 to 12:30.

Please bring your own gloves. The garden will provide soup for everyone.

A tool sharpener will be on site from 10:00, so do bring any tools that need attention. The water will also be shut off for the winter during the morning, so please finish any watering before then.

We hope to see you there.

The committee
