# Alder Lane Community Garden

## House rules for anything written to members

Apply these every time, without being reminded:

- Dates in full, with the weekday and year: Saturday 14 November 2026.
- Times on the 24-hour clock: 09:30, 14:00.
- British spelling (colour, programme, organise).
- No exclamation marks.
- Under 120 words.
- Sign off as "The committee". Never use personal names, including in the body.

Check the finished text against this list before saving it.
