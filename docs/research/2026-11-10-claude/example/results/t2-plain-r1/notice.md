# Autumn work day: Saturday 14 November

Dear members,

The garden's autumn work day is this **Saturday, 14 November, from 09:30 to 12:30**. Please come and help get the garden ready for winter.

- **Bring gloves.** The club supplies soup.
- **Tool sharpening:** the tool sharpener will be at the garden from 10:00, so bring any tools you would like sharpened.
- **Compost:** the new compost has arrived and is in the bays, ready for the beds.
- **Water:** the water will be shut off for the winter on the work day, so there will be no water at the garden after Saturday.

The garden closes for the winter at the end of November.

We hope to see you there.

The committee
Alder Lane Community Garden
