# Autumn work day: Saturday 14 November

Dear members,

The garden's autumn work day is **Saturday 14 November 2026, 09:30 to 12:30**, at the garden. Please come and help get the garden ready for winter.

- **Bring gloves.** The club will supply soup.
- **Tool sharpening:** a tool sharpener will be at the garden from 10:00.
- **Compost:** this autumn's delivery is in the bays.
- **Water:** the water will be shut off for the winter on the work day.

The garden closes for the winter at the end of November.

We hope to see you there.

The Alder Lane Community Garden committee
