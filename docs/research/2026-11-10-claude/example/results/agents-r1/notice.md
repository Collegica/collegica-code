# Autumn work day: Saturday 14 November 2026

Dear members,

The autumn work day is on Saturday 14 November 2026, from 09:30 to 12:30, at the garden. Please come along and help get the garden ready for winter.

- Bring gloves.
- The club will supply soup.
- A tool sharpener will be with us from 10:00, so bring any tools you would like sharpened.

The water will be shut off for the winter on the day itself, so there will be no water on site after that.

The garden closes for the winter at the end of November.

We look forward to seeing you there.

The committee
