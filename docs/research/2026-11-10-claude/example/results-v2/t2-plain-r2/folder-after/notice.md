# Alder Lane Community Garden: Autumn Work Day

**Saturday 14 November 2026, 09:30 to 12:30**

Dear members,

Please join us for the autumn work day at the garden this Saturday. This is our last big get-together before we close the garden for winter at the end of November.

**What to bring**

- Gloves.
- Any garden tools you'd like sharpened. A tool sharpener will be at the garden from 10:00.

**What we provide**

- Soup for everyone.

**Please note**

- The water will be shut off for the winter during the work day. If you want to water your plot or fill containers, do it before or early on Saturday.

Come for all three hours or just part of the morning. Every bit of help counts.

Thank you, and see you on Saturday.

Ibrahim Solheim, for the Alder Lane Community Garden committee
