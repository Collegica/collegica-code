# Autumn work day: Saturday 14 November

Dear members,

Our autumn work day is this **Saturday, 14 November, from 09:30 to 12:30** at the garden. We'll be getting the garden ready for winter before it closes at the end of November. Please come along, even if you can only stay for part of the morning.

**Please bring your own gloves.** The garden will provide soup.

**Tool sharpening:** a tool sharpener will be there from 10:00. Bring along any garden tools you'd like sharpened.

**Water shut-off:** the water will be turned off for the winter during the work day. After Saturday there will be no water at the garden, so fill any cans you need before then.

**Compost:** the new compost came on 15 October and is in the bays.

See you on Saturday,

The Alder Lane Community Garden committee
