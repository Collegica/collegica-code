# Autumn work day: Saturday 14 November 2026

Dear members,

Our autumn work day is on Saturday 14 November 2026, from 09:30 to 12:30, at the garden. Please come along for as much of the morning as you can.

- Bring your own gloves.
- Soup is provided.
- A tool sharpener will be there from 10:00, so bring any blunt tools.
- The water will be shut off for the winter on the day. Please fill any cans you need before then.

We close the garden for the winter at the end of November.

We look forward to seeing you there.
