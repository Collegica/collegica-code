# Alder Lane Community Garden: autumn work day

**Saturday 14 November 2026, 09:30 to 12:30**

Dear members,

Please join us for the autumn work day as we prepare the garden for winter.
The garden closes for the season at the end of November.

- Please bring gloves. The club will provide soup.
- A tool sharpener will arrive at 10:00. Bring any tools that need an edge.
- The water will be turned off for the winter during the work day, so please
  finish any watering before then.

Come for as long as you can. Every pair of hands helps.

The committee
