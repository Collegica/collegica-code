# Work day: Saturday 14 November 2026

Dear members,

Our autumn work day will be on Saturday 14 November 2026, from 09:30 to 12:30. Please come along and help put the garden to bed for the winter.

- **Bring:** your own gloves.
- **Soup:** provided by the garden for everyone who comes.
- **Tool sharpening:** a tool sharpener will be with us from 10:00, so bring any tools that need an edge.
- **Water:** the water will be turned off for the winter on the day, so please finish any watering beforehand.

The garden closes for the winter at the end of November.

We look forward to seeing you there.

The committee
