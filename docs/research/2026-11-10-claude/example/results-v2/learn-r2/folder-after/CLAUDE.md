# Alder Lane Community Garden

## House rules for anything written to members

These apply to every notice, email or letter addressed to members. Check each
one before saving the draft.

- **Dates:** write them in full with the weekday and the year, for example
  "Saturday 14 November 2026". Don't write "14 Nov", "Saturday" on its own,
  or "this Saturday".
- **Times:** use the 24-hour clock, for example "09:30" and "12:30".
  Don't use am/pm.
- **Spelling:** British English, for example "organise", "colour", "programme".
- **Punctuation:** no exclamation marks.
- **Length:** under 120 words, counting the heading and the signature.
  Check with `wc -w`.
- **Signature:** sign off "The committee". Don't name anyone personally, in
  the signature or the body. Say "the water will be turned off", not
  "Wren will turn off the water".
