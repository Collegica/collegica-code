# Alder Lane Community Garden: Autumn Work Day

**Saturday 14 November 2026, 09:30 to 12:30**

Dear members,

Please join us for the autumn work day this Saturday as we get the garden
ready for winter. The garden closes for the season at the end of November.

- **Bring gloves.** The club will provide soup.
- **Tool sharpening:** a tool sharpener is coming at **10:00**. Bring any
  garden tools that need an edge.
- **Water shut-off:** Wren will turn off the garden's water for the winter
  on the day. The taps will be off from then on, so do any watering you
  need before Saturday.

Come for as long as you can. Every pair of hands helps.

See you on Saturday,

The Alder Lane Community Garden Committee
