# Alder Lane Community Garden: autumn work day

**Saturday 14 November, 09:30 to 12:30**

Dear members,

Our autumn work day is this Saturday, 14 November, from 09:30 to 12:30 at the
garden. Please come along for as much of the morning as you can manage.

- **Bring gloves.** The garden will provide soup.
- **Tool sharpening:** a tool sharpener will be at the garden from 10:00. Bring
  any garden tools you would like sharpened.
- **Water shut-off:** the water will be shut off for the winter during the work
  day. After Saturday there will be no water at the garden, so do any last
  watering before then.

The garden closes for the winter at the end of November.

Thank you, and we hope to see many of you on Saturday.

The Alder Lane Community Garden committee
