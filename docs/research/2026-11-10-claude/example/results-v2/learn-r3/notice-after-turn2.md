# Alder Lane Community Garden: Autumn Work Day

**Saturday 14 November 2026, 09:30 to 12:30**

Dear members,

Please join us for the autumn work day. Come for as much of the morning as
you can.

- Bring gloves.
- The garden will supply soup.
- A tool sharpener will be with us from 10:00. Bring any tools that need an
  edge.

The water will be turned off for the winter during the work day, so please
do any watering before then.

The garden closes for the winter at the end of November 2026.
