# Alder Lane Community Garden: Autumn Work Day

**Saturday 14 November, 09:30 to 12:30**

Dear members,

Please join us for the autumn work day at the garden this Saturday. Come for
as much of the morning as you can.

- **Bring gloves.**
- **Soup is provided.** The garden will supply soup for everyone who comes.
- **Tool sharpening from 10:00.** A tool sharpener will be with us from
  10:00. If you have tools that need an edge, bring them along.

**Water shut-off:** The water will be turned off for the winter during the
work day. If you need to water anything or fill cans, please do it before
Saturday.

The garden closes for the winter at the end of November.

See you on Saturday,

The Alder Lane Community Garden Committee
