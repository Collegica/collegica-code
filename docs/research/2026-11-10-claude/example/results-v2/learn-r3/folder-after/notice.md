# Autumn work day: Saturday 14 November 2026

Dear members,

Our autumn work day is on Saturday 14 November 2026, from 09:30 to 12:30, at the garden. We will be getting the plots and shared areas ready for winter.

Please bring your own gloves. The club will supply soup.

A tool sharpener will be with us from 10:00, so bring any blunt tools you would like sharpened.

The water will be shut off for the winter on the day, so please finish any watering beforehand. The garden closes for the season at the end of November.

We hope to see many of you there.

The committee
