# Alder Lane Community Garden: committee files

## House rules for anything written to members

These apply to every notice, email or letter sent to members. Check each
one before saving a draft.

- **Dates:** write them in full with the weekday and the year, e.g.
  "Saturday 14 November 2026". Never "14 November" or "Sat 14/11".
- **Times:** use the 24-hour clock, e.g. "09:30 to 12:30", "17:00".
  No am/pm.
- **Spelling:** use British spelling (e.g. organise, colour, centre).
- **Punctuation:** no exclamation marks.
- **Length:** under 120 words in total, counting the heading and the
  signature. Count with `wc -w` before you finish.
- **Signature:** sign "The committee". Never put personal names in the
  signature or in the body, including committee members' names.
