# Alder Lane Community Garden: Autumn Work Day

**Saturday 14 November 2026, 09:30 to 12:30**

Dear members,

Please join us for the autumn work day at the garden. The garden closes for winter at the end of November.

**Please bring:** your gloves.

**We provide:** soup for everyone who comes.

**Tool sharpening:** a tool sharpener will be at the garden from 10:00. Bring along any garden tools you would like sharpened.

**Water shut-off:** the garden's water will be turned off for the winter on the work day, so there will be no water at the garden after that.

Come for as much or as little of the morning as you can. Every pair of hands helps.

See you there,

The Alder Lane Community Garden Committee
