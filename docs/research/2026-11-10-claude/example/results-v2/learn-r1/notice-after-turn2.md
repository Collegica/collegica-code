# Alder Lane Community Garden: Autumn Work Day

**Saturday 14 November 2026, 09:30 to 12:30**

Dear members,

Please join us for the autumn work day. The garden closes for winter at the end of November 2026.

**Please bring:** your gloves.

**We provide:** soup for everyone who comes.

**Tool sharpening:** a tool sharpener will be there from 10:00. Bring any tools you would like sharpened.

**Water:** the water will be shut off for the winter on the work day, so there will be no water at the garden after that.

Come for as much of the morning as you can.
