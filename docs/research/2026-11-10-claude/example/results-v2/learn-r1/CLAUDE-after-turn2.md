# Alder Lane Community Garden: committee files

## House rules for anything written to members

These apply to every notice, email or letter addressed to members. Check a draft against each rule before saving it.

- **Dates:** write them in full, with the weekday and the year, e.g. "Saturday 14 November 2026". Never write "14 Nov", "the 14th" or a date without its weekday and year.
- **Times:** use the 24-hour clock, e.g. "09:30" and "12:30". Never use "9:30am".
- **Spelling:** use British spelling ("colour", "organise", "programme").
- **Punctuation:** no exclamation marks.
- **Length:** under 120 words, counting the heading and the signature. Count with `wc -w` before saving.
- **Signature:** sign it "The committee". Don't add personal names anywhere in the notice, including the signature and the body (e.g. "Wren will…" or "Ibrahim is sending…").
