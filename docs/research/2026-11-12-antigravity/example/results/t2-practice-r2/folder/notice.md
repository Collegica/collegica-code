# Autumn Work Day Notice

Our autumn work day will take place on Saturday 14 November 2026 from 09:30 to 12:30, as we prepare the garden and put the beds to sleep for winter. Four cubic metres of fresh compost are in the bays ready for use.

Please bring your own gloves. The club will supply soup for everyone attending. A tool sharpener will also visit at 10:00, so feel free to bring any garden tools that need sharpening.

Please note that the garden water will be shut off for the winter during the work day, ahead of the garden closing at the end of November.
