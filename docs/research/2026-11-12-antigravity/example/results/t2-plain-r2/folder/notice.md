# Alder Lane Community Garden — Autumn Work Day Notice

**Date:** Saturday, 14 November 2026  
**Time:** 09:30 – 12:30  

Dear Members,

Please join us for our autumn work day on **Saturday, 14 November, from 09:30 to 12:30** as we prepare the garden for winter before closing at the end of November.

### Details for the Day

- **What to bring:** Please bring your own gardening gloves (and any tools you would like sharpened).
- **Refreshments:** The club will supply warm soup for everyone.
- **Tool sharpening:** A tool sharpener has been booked and will be on site starting at **10:00**.
- **Water shut-off:** Wren will shut off the garden's water supply for the winter on the work day itself, so please plan any final watering or wash-up accordingly.
- **Compost:** The fresh delivery of compost (four cubic metres) is in the bays and ready to use for putting your beds to sleep for the winter.

We look forward to seeing you there!
