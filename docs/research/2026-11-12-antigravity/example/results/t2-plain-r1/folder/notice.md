# Notice to Members: Autumn Work Day

**Alder Lane Community Garden**  
**Date:** Saturday, 14 November 2026  
**Time:** 09:30 – 12:30  

Dear Members,

Please join us for our autumn work day on **Saturday, 14 November**, from **09:30 to 12:30** as we prepare the garden for winter before it closes at the end of November.

### Details for the Day
- **What to bring:** Please bring your own **gloves** (and any garden tools you would like sharpened).
- **Refreshments:** The club will supply **soup** for everyone.
- **Tool sharpening:** A tool sharpener has been booked and will be at the garden starting at **10:00**.
- **Water shut-off:** Please note that Wren will be **shutting off the water for the winter** during the work day, so please plan any final watering accordingly.
- **Compost:** Four cubic metres of fresh compost have arrived and are in the bays ready for putting beds to sleep for the winter.

We look forward to seeing you there!
