# Autumn Work Day

The autumn work day at Alder Lane Community Garden will take place on Saturday 14 November 2026, from 09:30 to 12:30.

Please bring your own gloves; the club will supply soup. A tool sharpener has been booked and will arrive at 10:00, so you are welcome to bring tools that need sharpening.

Please also note that the water will be shut off for the winter on Saturday 14 November 2026 during the work day.
