# Alder Lane Community Garden — Autumn Work Day Notice

Dear Members,

Please join us for our autumn work day as we prepare the garden for winter closing:

- **Date:** Saturday, 14 November 2026
- **Time:** 09:30 – 12:30

## What to Know

- **What to bring:** Please bring your own gardening gloves.
- **Refreshments:** The club will provide soup for everyone.
- **Tool sharpening:** A tool sharpener has been booked and will be on site starting at **10:00**—feel free to bring any garden tools that need sharpening.
- **Water shut-off:** Please note that Wren will be shutting off the garden's water supply for the winter during the work day, so plan any final watering accordingly.
- **Compost:** Fresh compost is available in the bays for putting your beds to sleep before the garden closes for the winter at the end of November.

We look forward to seeing you there!
