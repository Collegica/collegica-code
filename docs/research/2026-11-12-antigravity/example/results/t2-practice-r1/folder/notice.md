# Autumn Work Day

Our autumn work day will take place on Saturday 14 November 2026 from 09:30 to 12:30.

Please bring your own gloves. The club will provide soup for everyone, and a tool sharpener will arrive at 10:00 if you have garden tools that need sharpening.

Please also note that the water supply will be shut off for the winter during the work day, and four cubic metres of fresh compost are in the bays for putting the beds to sleep before the garden closes at the end of November.
