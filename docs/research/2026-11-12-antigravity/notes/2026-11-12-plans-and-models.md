## Limits (Plans page)

- All plans: Gemini 3.8 Flash, unlimited Tab completions, all features
  including the CLI.
- Ultra: "The highest, most generous quota, refreshed every five hours";
  Pro: "A high, generous quota, refreshed every five hours until the weekly
  limit is reached"; others: "A meaningful quota, refreshed weekly".
- "the rate limits correlate with the amount of work done by the agent".
- Pro and Ultra can spend AI credits past the quota; the **AI Credit
  Overages** setting is *Never* or *Always*. In the CLI, `useG1Credits` in
  `settings.json`, or *Use AI Credits* in `/config` (credits page).
- No bring-your-own-key.
- `/usage` (alias `/quota`) shows the weekly and five-hour limits per model
  group (usage page).

## Eligibility (FAQ)

Personal Google Accounts on any plan, including free; 18 or over; listed
countries (Canada included). Workspace accounts need their administrator to
provision Antigravity.
