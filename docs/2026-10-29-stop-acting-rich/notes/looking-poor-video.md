## The seven reasons, as given

1. Radical contentment: "how much would make you happy" is always about
   twice what you have, at every income (unsourced).
2. Utility versus status: Morgan Housel's desert-island question, from
   *The Art of Spending Money* (as she cites it). "$5,000 inside my
   15-year-old purse" versus a $5,000 purse.
3. The visibility tax: looking rich invites higher prices. Anecdote: a
   tattoo-shop discount for a customer who left in a Ferrari.
4. Privacy and safety: flaunting wealth invites theft, scams and identity
   theft (sponsor segment).
5. Authentic relationships: filtering out "moochers"; MC Hammer's
   bankruptcy; her own family history.
6. Define rich yourself: groceries without checking prices; emergencies
   paid without debt. She and her husband paid off over $250,000 of debt in
   five years.
7. The hedonic treadmill; experiences over things.
