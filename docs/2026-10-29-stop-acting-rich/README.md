## Decisions

1. **Stanley's numbers are labelled US dollars, c. 2007 survey.** Sample size
   given as about 1,600 (his posts say 1,594 and 1,574).
2. **"Most millionaires live in homes under $300k" is not used**; Stanley's
   figure is 28.3%.
3. **Wine and watch medians are marked as less certain** (blog posts don't
   always name the survey).
4. **The Ramsey "Toyota top brand" claim is not used** (not verifiable from a
   fetchable Ramsey page); the Toyota finding is Stanley's.
5. **Our own arithmetic, labelled:** the share of Canadian millionaire
   families (30–33%, interpolated from SFS 2023 deciles), the credit card
   payoff table, and $500/month at 4% real (10 y $73,625; 25 y $257,065).
   FCAC's 36/84-month loan interest ($1,974/$4,681) was reproduced exactly.
